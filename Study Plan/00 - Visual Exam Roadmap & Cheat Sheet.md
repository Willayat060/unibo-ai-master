## 4. The 44-Day Attack Checklist for FAIKR Module 1 (Dec 21)

Because Module 1 is your nearest exam, print or check off these specific competencies:

- [ ] **BFS vs DFS vs UCS vs IDDFS**: Know the complete Time Complexity ($O(b^d)$) and Space Complexity proofs by heart.
- [ ] **$A^*$ Search Admissibility**: Prove why $h(n) \le h^*(n)$ guarantees optimality in tree search.
- [ ] **$A^*$ Search Consistency**: Prove why $h(n) \le c(n, a, n') + h(n')$ guarantees optimality in graph search without re-opening nodes.
- [ ] **Minimax & $\alpha$-$\beta$ Pruning**: Practice hand-tracing at least 5 game trees with pencil. Prune when $\alpha \ge \beta$.
- [ ] **Constraint Satisfaction (CSP)**: Hand-trace AC-3 arc-consistency on a graph. Apply MRV (Minimum Remaining Values) and Degree heuristics.
- [ ] **GraphPlan Mutexes**: Distinguish the 3 action mutexes (*Inconsistent Effects*, *Interference*, *Competing Needs*) and proposition mutexes (*Inconsistent Support*).
